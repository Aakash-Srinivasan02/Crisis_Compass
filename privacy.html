Crisis Compass — Privacy

Crisis Compass does not collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) from site visitors unless you voluntarily submit it. We do not track your location without your explicit permission. If you use the "Use my location" button, your browser will ask permission; location coordinates are used only in your browser session to filter results and are not sent to us.

Reports submitted via the anonymous report form are stored locally in your browser and intended for local admin review. If you want to submit sensitive information, please call the listed hotline numbers instead.

To host this site securely, run it over HTTPS. We recommend using a hosting provider with TLS (Let’s Encrypt or similar) or a platform that automatically provides HTTPS.
